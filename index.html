About Us

Hospitality Marketing Africa(HMA) is committed to elevating Africa's hospitality industry by connecting local businesses with global travelers. Our platform empowers hotels, restaurants, tour operators, and hospitality brands with the visibility and marketing support they need to thrive internationally.

Our vision is to build Africa's trusted alternative to global platforms like Booking.com and TripAdvisor - celebrating authentic African experiences while helping hospitality business grow.
